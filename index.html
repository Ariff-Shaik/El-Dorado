Seasonal Farming Tips

Test soil pH and nutrient levels before planting. Add organic compost to improve soil structure and fertility.

Implement drip irrigation for water conservation. Monitor soil moisture to avoid over/under watering.

Use integrated pest management (IPM) techniques. Rotate pesticides to prevent resistance development.
